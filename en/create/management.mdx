## What this page is for

After entering the creator area, the top of the page shows the **script list** and its current count. This is the entry point for everything: create a draft, search scripts, filter by status, continue an edit, and open published data.

## What each card shows

Each card usually contains:

- **Cover and title**: used to identify the work; without a cover, the card shows the first character of the title;
- **Status**: **Draft**, **In Review**, **Published**, or **Unpublished**;
- **Series or chapter information**: series works show the series and chapter;
- **Version label**: published works show the current version;
- **Time**: drafts show the last save time, published works show the publish time, and works in review show the submission time;
- **More actions**: a draft can be edited or deleted; a work in review can open its draft; a published work can start a new draft from an existing version.

## Filter and search

Status filters do not modify the work; they only change what the list shows:

| Filter | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| All | You want every script. |
| Draft | You want to continue or clean up unfinished work. |
| In Review | You want to track a submitted version. |
| Published | You want the version players can play and its data. |
| Unpublished | You want to find a version removed from the catalog. |

Search can use the script title or series name. If no card appears, clear both the search text and the status filter before assuming the script is gone.

## Published data

Selecting a published card opens a data panel with:

- Play count;
- Average turns per play;
- Favorites;
- Likes;
- Completions.

The numbers in the screenshot are live data captured locally, not a product promise. The panel also keeps an **Estimated Income** area, but it clearly says it is not supported yet, so you cannot get an amount, split, settlement date, or withdrawal status from here.

## Revenue share: now and later

The management page currently has no usable revenue settlement data. If the platform formally opens creator revenue later, this page can grow to explain:

1. Which works or play activity produce revenue;
2. The currently shareable and already settled amounts;
3. The settlement cycle, status, and detail;
4. The withdrawal methods allowed by platform rules.

These details should wait for the platform's formal rules. Do not treat **Estimated Income** as money received, and do not estimate revenue from play count yourself.

## How management and the editor work together

- Enter the editor from a **Draft** card to fill fields and connections;
- After **Save** in the editor, the card keeps the last save time;
- After a passing test, **Submit for Review** from the editor changes the status to **In Review**;
- If a submission is returned, fix the draft and resubmit;
- After approval and publication, check the current version and its data from the management page.

The management page does not merge "submit for review" and "publish automatically" into one action.
