KaiYan stories start in the script catalog. Pick a story you like, read its details, and follow the page prompts into the game.

## Your first visit

1. Browse the catalog and open a script’s details.
2. Check the summary, tags, creator, and expected length. Choose **Start New Game** or load an existing save.
3. Once inside, type what your character says or does. Open **Recommended Actions** when you are unsure how to continue.
4. If you run out of action value, open the recharge page to check your balance and top-up options.
