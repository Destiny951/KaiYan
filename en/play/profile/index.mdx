## Open your profile

Select the profile entry in the top-right corner. The overview shows follows, followers, action value, coins, and the profile tabs.

  <img src="/images/player/profile-home.png" alt="Profile home with account overview and feature tabs" />
</Frame>

## What each tab is for

- **Home**: overview, favorites, and achievements.
- **Action Value**: balance, transactions, and recharge.
- **History**: stories you played recently.
- **Favorites**: scripts you saved.
- **Achievements**: achievements you earned.
- **Followers**: people who follow you.
- **Following**: creators you follow.
- **Invite**: copy or bind invite codes.
- **Settings**: theme and default model.

## Useful tabs

Use **History** to find a story and return to its details page to load a save.
