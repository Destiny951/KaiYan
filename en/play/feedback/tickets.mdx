## Create a ticket

Open **Contact Us** and select **New Ticket**.

<Frame caption="Chinese UI screenshot: Feedback page">
  <img src="/images/player/contact-list.png" alt="Feedback page with customer service, new ticket, and status filters" />
</Frame>

In **Submit a Problem**, fill in:

1. **Subject**: describe the problem in one sentence.
2. **Description**: include the page, your actions, the result, and what you expected.
3. **Additional Image**: attach a useful screenshot if needed. The current page allows one PNG, JPEG, or WebP image up to 4 MB.

<Frame caption="Chinese UI screenshot: new ticket form">
  <img src="/images/player/ticket-create.png" alt="New ticket form with subject, description, and image area" />
</Frame>

Select **Submit Ticket** when finished. Do not include passwords, exchange keys, or other private information. Before sharing an image, hide invite codes, email addresses, QR codes, and payment details.

## Check status and reply

The ticket list can be filtered by **All**, **Open**, **In Progress**, **Waiting for You**, **Resolved**, and **Closed**. Open a ticket to read its details and status.

<Frame caption="Chinese UI screenshot: ticket reply area">
  <img src="/images/player/ticket-reply.png" alt="Ticket details with reply field, image attachment, and send reply button" />
</Frame>

If customer service asks for more information, add it in the reply area and send it there.
