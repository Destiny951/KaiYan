## Open customer service

Select **Contact Us** in the top navigation to open Feedback. Then select **Add Customer Service** in the top-right corner to expand the available contacts.

Use [New Ticket](/en/play/feedback/tickets) when you need a record, an attachment, or an ongoing conversation. Hide QR codes, email addresses, and other contact details before sharing screenshots.

## Which entry to use

- Use **Add Customer Service** when you first need to know who to contact.
- Open a ticket when you need to describe steps, attach an image, or wait for a result.
- Reply in the existing ticket instead of opening duplicates.
