## Open the menu

On the game page, select the round menu button in the right status area. The menu opens over the story; close it to return to the same place.

## The five sections

- **Characters** shows people who have appeared and information you have unlocked.
- **Locations** lists places you have visited or discovered.
- **Logs** lets you reread earlier story content.
- **Intelligence** collects confirmed information.
- **Clues** collects evidence you have discovered.

The menu only reviews information already found. It does not make a choice or advance the story for you.

## When to use it

Open **Characters** when you forget who someone is, **Locations** when you lose track of the setting, and **Logs** when you want to reread the previous turn. Use **Intelligence** and **Clues** to organize an investigation.

The top bar also provides direct access to logs and location, a display mode switch, and **Exit Game**.
